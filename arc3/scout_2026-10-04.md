## Ranking (expected gain × confidence, minus risk)
1. **Keep fork v2 (3600 s grace) as the safe final.** It protects against the 0.00 slow-load failure, which is worth more than any tweak.
2. **D' slot priority (candidate prepared).** Possible gain of +0 to 4 points; the evidence is weak (n=1).
   - Implementation risk is low. The installer checks that every scheduler name exists (all are present in our patch), and the
     embedded code only does math plus a monkeypatch.
   - Behavior risk is medium: with fresh-first removed, a hidden game could start late. Fresh games get the highest bonus
     (B=16 at level 1), so a game that never starts is unlikely.
   - Test: one demo run to check that `#OURS_FORM ok` appears and that no game has zero actions, then LB draws. Draws do not
     resolve ±4 points, so pick the final by both draws plus the demo.
3. **More streams (ARC3_MAX_ACTIVE_STREAMS 10 to 12-14) for throughput.** The other milestone stacks reach about 20% higher peak
   tok/s at 14-16 streams. Franzen's KV pool (1.01M tokens at 128k context) is already oversubscribed at 10 streams, though, so
   this could thrash. Needs a demo A/B on gen tok/s and levels first; no LB evidence.
4. Franzen's switches that are off (obstacledeveloper) are not recommended; his ablations showed no gain.
5. Sigeward's 7 min startup is harmful; do not use it.
