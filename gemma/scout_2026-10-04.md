## 3. Harness change that matters: wheelhouse of Sep 30 (adk_submission 0.2.12)

The source of `resolvers/generation.py` was checked in the 0.2.12 wheel. Discussions 744354 and 745059 describe the same change.

- `reasoning_content` is now mirrored to `reasoning`. Thoughts therefore persist across tool calls; before the update, vLLM dropped them.
- `thinking_budget` is forwarded as `extra_body.thinking_token_budget`.
- `include_thoughts: false` still sets `enable_thinking=False`. So R12, hsiaosuan and every top public bundle still run **without thinking**.

The jumps to 0.17 and 0.24 appeared on Oct 3-4, after this fix. Gluzdov's "Measure before you tune" notebook also says the method behind them is not public. Turning thinking on, now that it works, is the most plausible explanation that no public bundle has tested. It is a hypothesis with no evidence yet.
